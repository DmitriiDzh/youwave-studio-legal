YT Manager — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

YT Manager is a private, self-hosted tool. It is used by one person, its owner, to publish the owner's own short videos from the owner's own YouTube channels to the owner's own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest, X). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the tool uses

When the owner connects one of the owner's accounts, the platform gives the tool an access token for that account and basic profile information (account id, name, avatar). The tool uses them only to upload the owner's videos with their captions and to check that a post was published. It does not read followers' data, messages or any other people's information.

Where the data is kept

Tokens and settings are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties, sent to analytics or advertising services, or used to train AI models. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the platform the owner chose to post to.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect an account at any time in the tool's settings; the token is then deleted from the computer. Access can also be revoked in the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).
